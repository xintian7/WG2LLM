Key Principles for Responsible AI Use

1Human accountability

Authors remain fully responsible for all content. AI-assisted output must be critically reviewed, justified, and owned by expert authors.

2Transparency and traceability

AI use must be disclosed and documented. Outputs must be verified by experts and remain traceable for review and validation.

3Confidentiality and data protection

Do not input sensitive or unpublished material into third-party AI tools without appropriate safeguards and agreements.

4Comprehensiveness

AI-assisted assessments should consider the full breadth of relevant evidence and perspectives without bias.
